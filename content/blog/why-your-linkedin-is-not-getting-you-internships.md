I thought students didn't know LinkedIn... I was wrong.

Vanakkam guysss, Boo here again.

Sorry again for being late to your inbox 😅 I know I said I'll send regularly… but yeah, still fixing that habit.

But this time, I had to write this.

Over the last few weeks, I've been taking LinkedIn sessions for college students. Spoke to 100+ students easily.

And at first, I thought the problem was simple: students don't know how to use LinkedIn.

But then I actually looked at their profiles. And I was wrong.

Almost everyone had a LinkedIn profile. Good photo. Some details filled. Some even had skills listed.

But still… nothing was happening. No replies. No reach. No opportunities.

So I kept asking myself: if they already have a profile… why is it not working?

Then it hit me.

**The problem is not learning LinkedIn. The problem is using LinkedIn.**

Most students treat LinkedIn like a one time setup. Create profile. Add details. Done. And then they disappear.

But LinkedIn doesn't work like that anymore.

Today, LinkedIn is simple:

* If you don't show up, you don't exist.
* If you don't post, nobody notices you.
* If you don't stay active, nothing happens.

That's when I decided to try something different. Not another session. Something where people actually take action.

So I'm running a small LinkedIn Bootcamp for Students.

This is not going to be theory. You'll actually sit and do the work.

In 3 days, you will:

* Fix your profile properly
* Understand how to position yourself
* Write and publish your first post

And then comes the real part: 21 days of execution.

Because starting is easy. Continuing is where most people fail.

I'm keeping this small. Around 30 people only.

And yes, it's paid, ₹199. Not for the content, but to make sure people who join actually take it seriously.
