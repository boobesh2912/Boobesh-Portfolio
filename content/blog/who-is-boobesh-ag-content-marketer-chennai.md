**The short answer:** Boobesh AG, called Boo, is a content marketer and young entrepreneur from Chennai. He founded the content marketing agency [Gari Tech](/gari-tech) in February 2024, is Marketing Manager at Your College Senior, markets Proof, writes for StoryIt, and studies B.Tech Computer Science and Business Systems at Panimalar Engineering College.

That is me, so the rest of this is in first person. People search my name and land on bits and pieces. This is the one page that puts it together.

## The one line version

I am a college student in Chennai who started reselling online at 15, turned a Canva shop into a content and web studio, and now spends most days writing content for other people's brands.

## What I do

My work sits in content marketing:

- Content strategy and calendars
- Instagram Reels scripts
- LinkedIn posts for founders and personal brands
- Newsletters
- Turning one long video into a week of posts
- Brand positioning, which is working out what a brand should be known for before it posts anything

Through Gari Tech I also build WordPress websites, so if a project needs a landing page I can make it myself instead of waiting on someone else.

If you want the plain version of what a content marketer does, I wrote it down in [what is content marketing](/blog/what-is-content-marketing).

## Gari Tech, in a paragraph

Gari Tech started in February 2024, in my first year of college, as a small design shop in Canva. It is now a content marketing agency in Chennai that does content, social media, branding and websites for startups, small businesses and personal brands. It has six Google reviews, all five stars, and the websites it built, such as Speak With Ikigai, Pranav Mahi and Essential Counselling Center, are live. The website is [garitech.co.in](https://garitech.co.in), and the story of how it began is in [how I started Gari Tech](/blog/how-i-started-gari-tech-student-entrepreneur-chennai).
