**The short answer:** I started [Gari Tech](/gari-tech) in February 2024, in my first year at Panimalar Engineering College in Chennai. It began as a small design shop that made things in Canva, and it grew into a content marketing and web agency. This is how, including the parts I am not proud of.

## Before Gari Tech

I have been near technology since I was small, and I have always liked opening things to see what is inside. In 10th standard I searched for how to make money online, found reselling, and started selling products to strangers on Sharechat. The money was small. The lesson was not.

## The Canva shop

In first year I wanted to start something again, so I opened a small design shop, making things in Canva. Later a friend, Charan Kumar, joined, we added more services and split the work.

## The lie about WordPress

When we added web development, my friend took marketing and I said I would take design and web. There was one small problem. I had never opened WordPress in my life, but I had already told him I knew it.

So, technically, I lied. Then I fixed it. I asked my parents for a laptop, and they bought it. I asked for hosting and a domain, and they gave me both. My parents rarely say no when it is about learning. Only after buying the hosting did I install WordPress for the first time and start from zero. I do not recommend the order, but I cannot argue with the result. Several websites later, I can build them properly.

## Third year, and a split

In my third year my friend and I decided to go in different directions. He is more into marketing and started his own thing, and I am happy for him. It let me go deeper into Gari Tech. Since then, most of our clients have come through referrals and trials.

## What I would tell another student founder

1. **Sell before you are ready.** You will learn faster with a real client waiting.
2. **Tell people what you can do, then make it true fast.** I do not recommend the lie. I do recommend the speed.
3. **Ask for the small yes.** Parents, friends and first clients are usually more helpful than you expect.
4. **Learn to finish.** I have more than 50 projects on my drive, and many are unfinished. Gari Tech is one I kept going with.
5. **Stay consistent past week two.** Everything that has worked for me worked after the excitement wore off.

## What it looks like now

Gari Tech does content marketing, social media, Reels, Meta Ads support, branding and WordPress websites. Clients have left six Google reviews, all five stars, and I have collected the projects in [Gari Tech case studies](/blog/gari-tech-case-studies).
