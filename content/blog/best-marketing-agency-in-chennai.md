## Six questions that work on any agency

1. **Who will actually do my work?** Many agencies sell you with the founder and hand you to a junior. Ask who writes, who designs and who answers your messages.
2. **Can I see work like mine?** Not the biggest logo in their deck. A client at your size, in your kind of business.
3. **What will you need from me, and how often?** Content marketing stalls when the client cannot give feedback in time. A good agency tells you this upfront.
4. **How do you measure success?** Reach is easy to inflate. Ask about enquiries, sales conversations and what you would track together.
5. **What does it cost, and what is not included?** You want the whole investment explained before you sign, including ad spend, tools and revisions.
6. **What happens if we part ways?** You should own your accounts, your website and your files. If the answer is vague, walk away.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed rankings or guaranteed followers.
- No named people on the website.
- Case studies with no client name and no numbers you could check.
- A contract that locks in a long term before any trial work.
- Prices that appear only after a long sales call.

## Freelancer, small agency or large agency

A **freelancer** is the cheapest and the most flexible, and the most fragile. If they are ill or busy, your work stops. A **small agency** like Gari Tech gives you a few skills under one roof and direct access to the founder, at the cost of less capacity in a rush. A **large agency** gives you process and capacity, and you may be a small account to them.

None of these is better. Pick the one whose weaknesses you can live with.

## What does a marketing agency in Chennai actually do?

"Marketing agency" is a wide label, and a lot of confusion comes from that. Most agencies in Chennai sell some mix of the following.

- **Digital marketing.** The umbrella term for marketing done online: search, social media, email, paid ads and content.
- **Content marketing.** Deciding what you should say and producing it, so customers trust you before they buy. This is the centre of what [Gari Tech](/gari-tech) does.
- **Social media marketing.** Running your Instagram, LinkedIn or Facebook accounts, including posting, replying and design.
- **Performance marketing.** Paid campaigns on Google and Meta, judged by leads or sales.
- **SEO.** Making your website easier for search engines to find and understand.
- **Branding and design.** Your name, logo, colours, posters and the look of everything you publish.
- **Web development.** Building or rebuilding your website, often on WordPress.

Very few agencies are excellent at all seven. A good one tells you which two or three it is strongest at, and refers you elsewhere for the rest.

## Digital marketing agency, content marketing agency or social media agency?

Here is the plain way to choose.

- If you **do not yet know what to say**, you need a content marketing agency first.
- If you **know what to say but nobody sees it**, you need distribution: social media, SEO or paid ads.
- If you **need enquiries this month**, start with paid ads, and add content behind them so the ads convert better.
- If your **website is the problem**, fix the website before spending on anything else. Traffic sent to a weak site is wasted.

I explain the difference between the first two in [content marketing vs social media marketing](/blog/content-marketing-vs-social-media-marketing).

## How to compare proposals side by side

When two or three agencies send you proposals, put them in a simple grid and compare the same rows.

1. **Scope.** What exactly will be produced each month? Numbers of posts, videos, pages and revisions.
2. **Who does it.** Names or roles, not just "our team".
3. **Timeline.** When does the first piece go live, and when do you review results?
4. **Reporting.** What will you see, how often, and in what form?
5. **Ownership.** Do you own the accounts, the ad account, the website and every file at the end?
6. **Term and exit.** Is there a trial? How much notice is needed to stop?
7. **Total cost.** Including tools, ad spend, stock media and anything billed separately.

The cheapest proposal is rarely the best one, and neither is the longest. Pick the one where you understood every line.

## A simple scorecard you can copy

Score each agency from 1 to 5 on these, then add it up.

- Clarity of the first call
- Quality of work shown to you
- Relevance to your kind of business
- Honesty about limits
- Speed and quality of replies
- Fairness of the contract
- Whether you would enjoy working with them

The last one matters more than people admit. You will talk to these people every week.

## Questions to ask in the first call

Use these on every agency, including mine.

- Can I see two or three live examples for businesses like mine?
- What will you need from me each week, and what happens if I am slow?
- What is the first thing you would change about my current marketing?
- What would make you say this is not a good fit?
- Who owns the social accounts and the ad account?
- How do you report, and what do you do when a month goes badly?
- What have you tried that did not work, and what did you learn?

An agency that has a real answer to the last question has done real work.

## Local or remote?

A Chennai agency helps if you want to meet in person, shoot content on location, or work with people who understand the city, its festivals and its customers. Remote agencies can be excellent too. The location matters less than whether the agency understands your customer. If your customers are in Chennai, a team that lives here has an advantage.

## Budget: think in terms, not numbers

I am not going to quote prices here, because they depend on scope, and anyone who quotes a price without asking about scope is guessing. Instead, decide three things before you talk to anyone: what one new customer is worth to you, how many enquiries you would need per month to make the spend worthwhile, and how long you can afford to wait, since content marketing is slow. That gives you a sensible range to discuss.
