*This is the first issue of the Build With Boo newsletter, sent on 15 January 2026 to the Start The Up community.*

Hey hi,

I'm Boobesh (aka booooo) 👋

You're getting this mail because at some point, you shared your email in Start The Up community. Maybe for an event, a form, a session, or just out of curiosity. So yeah, this mail is not spam. You opted for it somewhere. 🙂

Let me tell you who I am, very honestly.

I'm a college student, just like you. I love exploring things. I try a lot of stuff. Some things work, many things fail. I'm that guy who is a jack of all trades, master of none.

Right now, I'm wearing many shoes at the same time:

* Founder of [GARI TECH](/gari-tech)
* Built a small Chrome extension called Focus Flow
* Doing content creation on Instagram and LinkedIn
* Running a student community called Start The Up
* And still figuring out life, money, direction, relationships, everything

I'm not writing this because I figured things out. I'm writing this because I haven't.

## Why I started this newsletter

Many of my friends keep asking me questions like:

* What should I study?
* What skill should I learn?
* How do I use college life properly?
* Everyone is doing something, I feel stuck
* Startup or Job?

Honestly, I don't have perfect answers. But I have experience. Trying. Failing. Learning. Getting clarity slowly.

So instead of replying the same things again and again, I thought, why not write it down and share it with people like me?

That's how Build With Boo started.

## What you will get from this newsletter

Very simple and very real.

In future mails, I'll talk about things like:

* Money and earning as a student
* Direction and confusion in college life
* Startups, business, and career thoughts
* How to land a dream job
* Skills, growth, and mistakes
* And yes, relationships too, because nobody really talks about it honestly

I'll share what I know. I'll also share what I don't know.

No motivation speeches. No pretending to be successful. Only honest thoughts and clarity.
