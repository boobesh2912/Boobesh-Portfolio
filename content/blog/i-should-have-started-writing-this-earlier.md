Honestly… this is not a "newsletter shift" mail 😭

This is more like: "Okay, let me finally build something personal and real."

For the past few months, I've been posting content, building [GARI TECH](/gari-tech), building the Start The Up community, doing internships, freelancing, attending events, meeting founders, mentoring students, trying random ideas, failing in some, learning from some…

But one thing I realized recently: most people only post the highlight reel.

Nobody talks about:

* confusion in college
* feeling lost about career
* fake internships
* relationship thoughts while still in college
* pressure to "figure life out"
* failed ideas
* burnout
* overthinking
* trying to balance studies + building something

And honestly… naanum adhe phase la dhan iruken.. or maybe irunthn…

Still figuring things out. Still making mistakes. Still learning daily.

That's why I've started writing on Substack, a more personal platform where creators share honest thoughts, stories and detailed thoughts beyond normal social media posts.

There, I'll share:

* mistakes I make so you can avoid them

* free resources and tools
* random clarity moments I get while building stuff 😭
* startups, entrepreneurship, jobs

Basically… everything I wish someone openly told me earlier, which maybe makes my life even easier.

A lot of people know me through:

* GARI TECH

* content creation
* LinkedIn <3

But this space is more personal.

No fake productivity. No "earn 10 lakhs in 10 days" nonsense. No pretending life is perfect. Just honest building in public.

If you feel like you're also trying to become "something more" during college… you'll probably relate to this :)
