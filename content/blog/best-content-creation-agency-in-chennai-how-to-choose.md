## What "content creation" actually includes

When someone searches for a content creation agency, they usually mean some mix of:

Make a list of which of these you really need, so you compare like with like.

## Content creation vs content marketing

Content creation is making the content. Content marketing is deciding what to make, for whom and in what order, then making sure it gets published and reviewed. Many agencies sell the first and call it the second. For a small business, strategy plus creation together usually works better than creation alone. I explained the difference in [content marketing vs social media marketing](/blog/content-marketing-vs-social-media-marketing).

## How to compare content creation agencies

Score each agency from 1 to 5 on these:

1. **Work you can open.** Live content, live websites, real case studies.
2. **Reviews you can verify.** Google reviews or a client you can message.
3. **Who creates your content.** The person you meet, or someone you never see?
4. **What is included.** Number of posts, videos, designs, rounds of changes.
5. **Process.** How ideas, approvals and publishing work.
6. **Honesty.** Do they tell you what will not work?
7. **Fit.** Have they worked with a business like yours?
8. **Price clarity.** A written quote with what is included and what costs extra.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed followers, reach or virality.
- Only mock ups and no live work.
- Nobody can say who will create your content.
- No written scope.
- Pressure to sign quickly.
- Reports that are all likes and no explanation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is the best content creation agency in Chennai?**
There is no single best for everyone. Compare agencies on live work, verifiable reviews, who creates your content and a written scope. Gari Tech is one option among others.

**What does a content creation agency do?**
It writes, designs, films or edits content for your brand. A good one also plans what to make and reviews what worked.

**How much does content creation cost in Chennai?**
It depends on how much you need and in which formats. Ask for a written quote listing what is included and what costs extra.

**Can a small business afford a content creation agency?**
Yes, with a small scope. Start with one channel and a clear plan, then expand when it works.

**What is the difference between a content creation agency and a social media agency?**
A social media agency manages and posts on your accounts. A content creation agency makes the content, and a content marketing agency also plans and reviews it.
